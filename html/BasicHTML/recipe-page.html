Enjoy the Authentic Flavor of Homemade Fried Rice

Who can resist the enticing aroma of freshly cooked fried rice? As one of the most popular and beloved dishes, fried rice is the perfect blend of simplicity and rich flavors. Whether enjoyed as a quick breakfast, a comforting dinner, or a clever way to transform leftover rice, this dish never fails to satisfy. With simple seasonings like garlic, sweet soy sauce, eggs, and your favorite toppings, you can create a restaurant-quality meal right in your own kitchen. Follow this easy recipe to make delicious, savory, and satisfying fried rice at home!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cold cooked white rice (preferably leftover rice)
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 4 shallots, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp sweet soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp chicken or mushroom bouillon powder
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • Optional toppings: Sliced sausages, meatballs, shredded chicken, chopped green onions

Instructions

  1. Heat cooking oil in a skillet over medium heat. Crack in the eggs, scramble until cooked, then push to the side of the pan.
  2. Add shallots and garlic, sautéing until fragrant.
  3. Add optional toppings such as sausages, meatballs, or shredded chicken and stir-fry briefly.
  4. Add the cold rice, breaking up any clumps so the grains separate easily.
  5. Pour in sweet soy sauce, soy sauce, salt, pepper, and bouillon powder.
  6. Turn to high heat and stir-fry rapidly until seasonings are thoroughly combined and fragrant.
  7. Toss in chopped green onions, remove from heat, and serve warm.
